Broiler Farm Record Management System

An offline-first mobile app and web console that replaces the paper batch book and the spreadsheet with one real-time system — built for a working poultry farm, not a classroom mockup.

Built for the farm, not just the demo

What it actually does

Works with no signal

Staff keep recording in the field with no connection. Everything queues on the device and reaches the server the moment it reconnects, or with a manual sync.

Live financials, not post-mortem

Break-even price, profit/loss and Feed Conversion Ratio recalculate as entries come in, not once a batch is finished and manually totalled.

Role-based access

Four roles, each seeing and doing only what belongs to them — enforced at the database, not just hidden in the interface.

One record of truth

Mortality, feed, weight, vaccination, harvest, expenses and sales all live in one place instead of across a notebook and a spreadsheet.

Every bird accounted for

Mortality and harvest counts are validated against the starting flock size, so the numbers can't quietly drift apart.

Closes itself out cleanly

Once mortality and harvest account for the full flock, the Manager gets prompted to close the batch — reversible, and never silent.

A batch, start to finish

How a flock moves through the system

01 — Register

Manager opens a batch with its starting bird count and, optionally, assigns a Staff member on the spot.

02 — Record

Staff log mortality, feed, weight and vaccinations daily, from the farm, with or without a connection.

03 — Harvest

Harvests are recorded by count, then priced by the Manager — per kilogram, as a flat total, or given away at zero.

04 — Close

Once the numbers account for every bird, the Manager closes the batch. Profit, break-even and FCR are already there, not calculated after the fact.
