Four roles, one system

Who it's built for

Office roles work in the web console; field roles work in the Android app, online or off. Each role sees and does only what belongs to them.

Super Admin

Owns the platform

One level above every business on the system.

Web console · online only

  • Creates Admin accounts — access is invite-only, never self-serve.
  • Deactivates an Admin when the business relationship ends.
  • Reactivates the same account later without losing anything.

Deactivation is reversible by design, and enforcement lives in the database — Supabase row level security — not only in the interface.

Admin

Owns the business

Registers the farm and staffs it up.

Web console · online only

  • Registers the business and its details.
  • Adds the Manager and up to 18 Staff to that business.
  • Views every batch read-only — visibility without editing power.

An Admin manages people and visibility. The day-to-day farm numbers stay with the Manager and Staff.

Manager

Runs the operation

Turns a flock into a batch, and a batch into a profit figure.

Android app · offline-first

  • Registers flock batches with a starting bird count.
  • Assigns Staff to batches, and can assign on the spot.
  • Logs expenses: feed, drugs, labour and other batch costs.
  • Prices harvests per kilogram, as a flat total, or as a gift at zero.
  • Sees the financial summaries: break-even, profit or loss, FCR.
  • Closes a batch once every bird is accounted for — reversible.

One active Manager per business keeps ownership of the numbers unambiguous.

Staff

Works the field

Records the day, in the house, with or without signal.

Android app · offline-first

  • Records mortality, feed, weight samples, vaccinations and harvests.
  • Works on their assigned batch only.
  • Saves on the device first, then syncs automatically when signal returns.

The home screen always shows connection status and how many entries are still pending, and a manual sync is one tap away.

Built for the farm, not just the demo

What it actually does

Six things that decide whether a record book survives contact with a real farm.

Works with no signal

Staff keep recording in the field with no connection. Everything queues on the device and reaches the server the moment it reconnects, or with a manual sync. The home screen shows whether you are online and how many entries are still waiting.

Mortality Feed Weight
On-device first Auto sync on reconnect Pending count

Live financials, not post-mortem

Break-even price, profit/loss and Feed Conversion Ratio recalculate as entries come in — not once a batch is finished and manually totalled.

Role-based access

Four roles, each seeing and doing only what belongs to them — enforced at the database with Supabase row level security, not just hidden in the interface.

One record of truth

Mortality, feed, weight, vaccination, harvest, expenses and sales live in one place instead of across a notebook and a spreadsheet.

Every bird accounted for

Mortality plus harvested can never exceed the starting flock, so the numbers cannot quietly drift apart.

Closes itself out cleanly

Once mortality and harvest account for the full flock, the Manager is prompted to close the batch — reversible, and never silent.

How it is worked out. Break-even price = (total expenses − revenue collected so far) ÷ birds still to harvest. Dead birds are excluded from the denominator, but their cost stays in the numerator. Once a batch is already profitable the price is clamped at ₦0, and when no birds remain to price, the result is —.

    How a flock moves through the system

    Four steps, in order, from an opening bird count to a closed batch with the numbers already there.

    1. 01
      Step one

      Register

      The Manager opens a batch with its starting bird count and, optionally, assigns a Staff member on the spot. Everything after this is measured against that number.

      Starting flockStaff assignment
    2. 02
      Step two

      Record

      Staff log mortality, feed, weight and vaccinations daily, from the farm, with or without a connection. Each entry saves on the device first, then syncs.

      MortalityFeedWeightVaccinations
    3. 03
      Step three

      Harvest

      Harvests are recorded by count, then priced by the Manager — per kilogram, as a flat total, or given away at zero.

      CountPer kg or flat total
    4. 04
      Step four

      Close

      Once the numbers account for every bird, the Manager closes the batch. Profit, break-even and FCR are already there, not calculated after the fact — and the close can be reversed.
